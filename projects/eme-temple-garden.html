leading a government-collaborated public space revamp at the eme temple in vadodara, developed as a csr initiative alongside hardscape and softscape design for a separate 60,000 sq ft private garden for yash high voltage.

this thread runs through the csr vertical at incredible garden art: identifying underutilised urban spaces, developing design proposals, approaching government bodies for site approvals, and pitching projects to corporate clients as csr initiatives. the same period included landscape design for anita dongre's rewild 2026, a fashion event for animal welfare, and a jungle-themed event installation at vantara promoting sustainable, reusable living plant decor.
