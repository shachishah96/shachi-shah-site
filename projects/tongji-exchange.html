courtyard housing, tongji university exchange

participatory fieldworkethnographic observation
site
shanghai, china
method
observation, gamified fieldwork
barrier
full language gap

observational and conversational fieldwork on courtyard housing typologies, conducted while navigating a full language barrier through non-verbal communication, body language reading, and google translate.

a gamified wooden block model was developed and played with residents to surface their spatial behaviours, feelings, and relationships to shared space, a workaround for the language gap that ended up being more revealing than a standard interview would have been. findings from these sessions informed a participatory intervention proposal, combining urban studies with behavioural observation methods.

this exchange sits early in a pattern that shows up again later: when the direct route to understanding how people relate to space is blocked, build a different route in, an object, a game, a shared task, rather than force language to do all the work.
